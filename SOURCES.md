# Sources — news of Wednesday 18 November 2026

All six stories are the news of 18 November 2026 as the world's morning
editions reported it: events of the day itself, or first reported in the
18 November editions (Asia) after breaking late on 17 November UTC.
Facts are as reported; nothing is invented. The image itself is original
work: a front page drawn from scratch (HTML + inline SVG, screenshotted
locally).

1. **Knesset hands Netanyahu a defeat: Kahana elected speaker, 66–52** (lead)
   - The Knesset voted on Tuesday to elect Matan Kahana of Gadi Eisenkot's
     Yashar party as speaker, 66 votes to 52 for Likud's Ofir Katz, after
     a debate of more than seven hours; Kahana replaces Amir Ohana
     (Reuters, "Eisenkot-led bloc replaces Netanyahu ally as Knesset
     speaker", 17 Nov 2026; CNN, 17 Nov 2026; carried in 18 Nov editions).
   - Eisenkot, tasked by President Herzog with forming a government after
     the deadlocked 27 October election, called the vote "a proper
     democratic move which reflects the absolute majority who voted for
     change" (CNN; Reuters).

2. **APEC opens in Shenzhen**
   - The APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting opens in Shenzhen on Wednesday
     18 November and runs to 19 November (Reuters via BusinessWorld,
     17 Nov 2026; Pollar News timeline).
   - A first three-way meeting of Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir
     Putin is set for Thursday 19 November in Shenzhen on the APEC
     sidelines (China foreign ministry, via Outlook India, 17 Nov 2026;
     also in the 17 Nov entry's sources).

3. **U.S. and Iran trade fire**
   - Iran's Revolutionary Guard launched drones at a U.S. radar system at
     Sheikh Isa Air Base, Bahrain, on Tuesday; U.S. Central Command said
     the drones were intercepted with no damage or injuries, and U.S.
     fighter jets then struck an IRGC missile launch site on Qeshm Island
     with precision munitions (AP, via ListenUpYall, 17 Nov 2026; Al
     Jazeera live blog; U.S. Central Command, via WLT Report).
   - It was Iran's third attack on U.S. military targets in Bahrain in
     five days, ending a roughly two-month lull in fighting (AP).

4. **Cyclone Akvan turns severe**
   - The India Meteorological Department says Cyclonic Storm Akvan became
     a severe cyclonic storm on the morning of Wednesday 18 November and
     is forecast to become a very severe cyclonic storm by the evening of
     19 November (IMD National Bulletin No. 18, 22:30 IST 17 Nov 2026;
     IMD RSMC advisory, 17 Nov 2026).
   - Landfall is forecast around noon on Friday 20 November between
     Nellore and Visakhapatnam as a very severe cyclonic storm, with
     maximum sustained winds of 120–130 km/h gusting to 145 km/h; heavy
     rain is expected in coastal Andhra Pradesh from 18 November (IMD).

5. **SEC opens probe into Nvidia**
   - The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating Nvidia
     following the resignation of CEO Jensen Huang, focusing on whether
     the company's disclosures misled investors; sources told the New York
     Post on Tuesday (NY Post, 17 Nov 2026).
   - Three House committee chairs asked Huang to appear for a closed-door
     briefing on 18 November (NY Post; AIStockWire timeline).

6. **Dhoni calls time on IPL career**
   - MS Dhoni announced his retirement from IPL playing cricket and will
     become Chennai Super Kings mentor (Times of India / Education Times
     school-assembly headlines for 18 November 2026, published 17 Nov
     2026; The Daily Jagran, 18 Nov 2026).
